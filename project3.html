Graphic Design 2 Book Cover

In our Graphic Design 2 class, we were tasked to create a book cover. The book "Where the Crawdads Sing" by Delia Owens is a coming-of-age, suspense-filled novel that follows Kya and her challenges of family and relationships. During the process, we were challenged with creating studies using only shapes and typography to focus on the main themes of the book without using imagery. After understanding the themes thoroughly, I began to use imagery and typography in the finals to represent Kia's desire to free her soul and be with her true family, nature.

Shape Studies

These studies solely focused on shapes. I was tasked with using the shapes to represent the themes of judgment, isolation, and naturalism. I narrowed down to 4 ideas using only shapes.

Type Studies

These studies solely focused on typography. I was tasked with using the type to represent the themes of judgment, isolation, and naturalism. I finalized 4 ideas based on just typographic studies.
