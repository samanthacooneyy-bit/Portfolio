Typography II Social Campaign

For this project, we were tasked with creating a brand for a social campaign in our Typography II class. I designed a bold visual system to raise awareness about mental health in sports, tailored for high-impact public spaces. Starting with sketches, I developed two digital concepts before refining one. The final design transforms typography into sports equipment shapes like basketballs and tennis balls, making the message visually striking and instantly recognizable.

Concept 1

For these studies, I focused on the different sports and their main equipment. I used the typography to replace the lines of the equipment, so that if you look at the posters, they still read as their represented sport.

Concept 2

These are my second concept studies during my process, which I focused on boxing. I thought about the culture of boxing and the distinct look that boxing posters have, from which I got inspiration.

Sketches

These sketches consist of popular things in sports and my first concept of boxing, geared towards expressing the mentally tough side of the sport. I used jersey numbers, sports cards, and game statistics as a start for ideas. I narrowed it down to the final idea of using sports equipment lines alone.
